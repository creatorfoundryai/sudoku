1. Introduction

Creator Foundry AI ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile game Sudoku: Focus, Zen & Arcade. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the game.

2. Accounts and Information You Provide

The game does not require you to create an account, sign in, provide a username, or provide personal profile information.

Game progress and gameplay preferences are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated within the application.

3. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google, to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from network information, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and other device or usage information for purposes including delivering, measuring, securing, and improving advertising services.

The information processed and the purposes for which it is used may depend on your device, region, privacy settings, and consent choices.

For more information about how Google handles information, please see Google's privacy policies and information about Google's advertising services.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices relating to advertising.

In regions where consent is required, users may be presented with choices regarding the processing of personal data and personalized advertising. Where required, users can access privacy options from within the game to review or change their choices.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Creator Foundry AI does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics in this game.

Third-party services used by the game, including the advertising SDK, may process diagnostic, performance, advertising, and interaction information as described in their own documentation and privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

Creator Foundry AI does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers used to operate features of the game, particularly Google and its advertising partners for advertising-related purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and preferences stored locally by the game remain on the device until they are removed by the user, the application's data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, subject to platform behavior.

Creator Foundry AI does not operate a user account system for this game and does not maintain a server-side account database containing player profiles.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention policies of those service providers.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the information handled directly by the game and rely on established platform and service-provider security mechanisms for third-party services used by the application.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Sudoku: Focus, Zen & Arcade is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
